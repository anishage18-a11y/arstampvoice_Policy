ARS TempVoice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ARS TempVoice may process when it is used in a Discord server and how that information is used.

1. Information We May Process

To provide its features, ARS TempVoice may process Discord-related information such as server/guild IDs, channel IDs, user/member IDs, usernames or display names, voice-channel state information, and configuration data.

2. Temporary Voice Data

The bot may store information needed to operate temporary voice channels, including temporary channel IDs, ownership information, trusted or blocked member IDs, and saved voice-channel preferences such as channel name, privacy state, chat state, or user limit.

3. Configuration and Reports

Server configuration may include category, text-channel, voice-channel, reporting, and other bot settings. Information submitted through supported reporting or support features may be processed to handle the relevant request.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To create, manage, and remove temporary voice channels.
  • To enforce channel ownership and access settings.
  • To save server and user preferences required by the bot.
  • To provide commands, controls, support, and troubleshooting.
  • To maintain security and prevent misuse of the service.

5. Data Storage

ARS TempVoice may store operational data in configuration/database files used by the bot. Data retention depends on the bot's configuration and may continue until it is removed, reset, overwritten, or no longer required for the relevant feature.

6. What We Do Not Intend to Collect

ARS TempVoice is not designed to intentionally collect passwords, payment-card information, or private credentials. Users should not submit sensitive personal information through bot commands or support features unless specifically required and authorized.

7. Discord

ARS TempVoice interacts with Discord's API. Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own privacy policy, terms, and applicable settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Operational information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the bot and its hosting environment.

9. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect stored bot data. However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices

You may stop using ARS TempVoice by removing it from your server. Server administrators can also change or remove bot configuration. For a privacy-related request, contact the bot owner/developer through the official support channel.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the ARS TempVoice owner/developer through the official support server or support channel associated with the bot.
